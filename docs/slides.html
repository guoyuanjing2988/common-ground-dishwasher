1 · The problem

A small chore that never ends

  • Every day someone bends down, reaches in and loads the dishwasher.
  • Who it hurts most: older people living at home, the family members who care for them, busy two-job households.
  • Singapore context: an ageing population, compact kitchens, and countertop dishwashers that need no plumbing work.
  • Design rule: no renovation. Same counter, same appliance, a docking strip and a charger.

GPT (seconds)

  • task order from a goal in English or Chinese
  • skill parameters
  • diagnose and recover
  • when to ask a human

Robot (milliseconds)

  • 1 kHz torque, force control
  • speed caps near people
  • preconditions, collision checks
  • hardware e-stop

Example: the door springs back to 68°. GPT reads DOOR_NOT_LATCHED and retries holding the door 4 s at its stop. It latches.

Also: GPT commanded the whole mobile mission (3/3); as a tester it found 9 failure modes; as an engineer it fixed the plate lean, 9.7° → 2.1°.

6 · Real hardware & cost

Every chip has one job

  • Franka FR3 + Hand + wrist F/T; 1 kHz torque loop
  • Main PC (RT Linux, ROS 2): IK 100 Hz, skills, docking
  • STM32H723: wheels, brakes, battery, bumpers, watchdog
  • Jetson + RealSense on the wall: camera, Codex calls
  • 51.2 V 30 Ah LFP: ≈ 10 Wh per mission, ~95–120 missions per charge
  • PILZ safety relay: e-stop cuts arm and wheels with no software in the loop
  • BOM ≈ US$53k (≈ S$69k, 28 lines, incl. 10 % contingency); the arm + hand + F/T is 68 % of it

8 · Honest status & next

Simulation and design today. Real robot next.

  • ✓ Physics in MuJoCo: wheels, brakes, contacts, plate, pod, door, basket
  • ✓ Real GPT calls with real latency and real recoveries
  • ✓ A costed hardware design down to fuses and contactors
  • ✗ Not a physical robot yet. No water, washing or real touch sensing
  • ✗ The 5-dish runs start with the door open; small run counts mean feasibility, not reliability
  • Next 1: a US$25 conductive-fingertip test on the real E804 touch panel
  • Next 2: one run from the charger to a full load; destack piles robustly; build the base
  • Next 3: offline fallback (local behaviour tree), with GPT as the exception handler
  • Next 4: human confirmation before starting a wash, and verification sensors for steps GPT cannot see
